## ⚕️ Important Medical Notice

This project is an **open-source AI tooling framework** to help users explore Jan Aushadhi generic medicine equivalents. It does **not** provide direct medical advice, diagnosis, or prescription services. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before changing or substituting medications.
